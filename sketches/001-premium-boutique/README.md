## Variant: Premium Boutique

### Design stance
A warm boutique shop that feels buyer-ready and less like a developer portfolio.

### Key choices
- Layout: big editorial hero plus product card grid
- Typography: oversized but soft
- Color: cream, black, warm brown
- Interaction: scroll CTA and hover cards

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: trust, product browsing, professional feel
- Weak at: less gamer/accessory personality

### Best for
A real shop that wants to look established and calm.
