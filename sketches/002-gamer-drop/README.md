## Variant: Gamer Drop

### Design stance
A bold dark accessory/pendant storefront with energy and stronger category identity.

### Key choices
- Layout: glassy dark panels and drop-focused hero
- Typography: loud, compressed-feeling scale
- Color: black, purple, cyan, pink
- Interaction: filter active states and toast

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: accessory/pendant identity, excitement
- Weak at: less calm/professional, can feel more niche

### Best for
A shop whose buyers like gamer visuals and collectible drop culture.
