## Variant: Soft Accessory Boutique

### Design stance
A softer, more colorful take on Premium Boutique with accessory personality but no harsh dark gamer theme.

### Key choices
- Layout: boutique hero plus product image mosaic
- Typography: bold and friendly
- Color: soft pink, blue, cream, black
- Interaction: hover cards and strong CTAs

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: accessory identity, warmth, approachability
- Weak at: may feel slightly cute depending on target customers

### Best for
A shop aimed at younger Instagram/accessory buyers while staying clean.
